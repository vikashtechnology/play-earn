# Business Model

## Revenue

1.  Partner offers with verified conversions.
2.  Direct advertiser campaigns.
3.  Brand partnerships.
4.  Product/commerce margin or commission.
5.  Standard advertising placements.

## Unit Economics

``` text
Advertiser/partner revenue
- provider fees
- user reward cost
- payout/fulfillment cost
- fraud/reversal reserve
- operations/tax costs
= contribution margin
```

Keep reward liabilities matched to verified revenue or approved campaign
budgets.

## Coins

Use one internal coin unit with configurable redemption economics. Do
not promise unlimited cash value.
