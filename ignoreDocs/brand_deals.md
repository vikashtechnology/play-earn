# Brand Deals & Partnerships

## Partnership Types

-   Sponsored rewards
-   Sponsored products
-   Coins + cash product deals
-   Coupons/promos
-   Featured compliant placements
-   Affiliate/commerce campaigns

## Onboarding

``` text
Lead → qualification → verification → proposal → commercial terms
→ campaign setup → tracking → launch → reporting → settlement
```

## Campaign Fields

Brand, objective, budget, audience, dates, reward, conversion event,
attribution window, creative, landing destination and settlement terms.

## Product Fields

SKU, brand, product, partner cost, reference price, coin contribution,
cash contribution, shipping, stock, delivery SLA, return policy and
fulfillment provider.

## Commercial Models

Fixed fee, verified-action pricing, revenue share, sponsored inventory
or negotiated discount.

Use written commercial terms.
