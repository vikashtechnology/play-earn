# Compliance & Play Store Gate

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.

## Play Store

Review current requirements for target API, privacy policy, Data Safety,
account deletion, payments, loyalty/rewards, advertising SDKs and
offerwall/incentivized campaign behavior before release.

## Rewards

Show clear eligibility, reward terms, minimum withdrawal,
pending/reversal rules and support channels. Do not misrepresent
earnings.

## Payments/Payouts

Use verified business/provider accounts and complete required KYC. Do
not hold/transmit regulated funds without required authorization.

## Privacy

Collect necessary data only, secure it, document third-party SDK
collection and provide deletion/retention controls.

## India

Obtain professional review for applicable DPDP/privacy, consumer
protection, tax/GST/accounting, payment/payout, advertising and
e-commerce/fulfillment obligations.

## Release Gate

No production launch until product, provider, payment, payout and
compliance reviews are complete.
