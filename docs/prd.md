# PRD — India Rewards Marketplace

## Product

An India-first rewards platform where users earn virtual coins through legitimate activities, verified partner offers, surveys, referrals and platform campaigns, then redeem coins for eligible cash payouts, gift cards/vouchers, or physical products using coins + cash where supported.

## Target Users

- Indian users seeking rewards and offers.
- Advertisers seeking measurable campaigns.
- Brands seeking acquisition, loyalty and product-promotion campaigns.
- Admin/operations staff managing rewards, payouts, fraud and partnerships.

## MVP

- OTP authentication, profile and account deletion.
- Home, earn/tasks, offers, referrals and wallet.
- Pending/approved/reversed reward states.
- UPI/bank redemption where enabled.
- Gift-card/voucher redemption.
- Product redemption with coins + cash where supported.
- Transaction/order history, notifications and support.
- Admin: users, campaigns, wallet ledger, withdrawals, rewards, products, brands, fraud, analytics and CMS.

## Not in MVP

- Gambling, betting or chance-based cash games.
- User deposits for winning rewards.
- Unlicensed regulated financial-wallet functionality.
- Fake installs, fake engagement or conversion manipulation.
- Incentivized app-install activity as the sole/main Play Store functionality.
- Global launch, crypto rewards, or direct physical warehousing.

## Success Metrics

Retention, verified completion rate, approval/reversal rate, revenue per active user, withdrawal success, gift-card fulfillment, product-order completion, fraud rate, support resolution and contribution margin.

## Principles

Server-verified rewards, auditable ledger, visible pending balance, clear reward conditions, configurable provider adapters and fraud resistance.
