3. Sign-in and how your credentials are handled

  • Authentication is provided by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) using email link, Google sign-in, or phone OTP. Google verifies you and issues a short-lived signed token.
  • Our server independently verifies that token's signature against Google's published signing certificates before it trusts anything in it. We never accept an identity claimed by the app alone.
  • We then issue our own platform session token, valid for 15 minutes. Your device stores it encrypted with the Android Keystore (AES/GCM). We do not store a copy of your Firebase credential on your device or in our database.
  • Anonymous sign-in and custom tokens are refused, so every balance belongs to a verifiable identity.
  • Signing out revokes your platform sessions server-side.
  • Google processes authentication data under its own terms — see the Firebase/Google disclosures in section 6.

4. Advertising

The app shows rewarded video advertising through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers, including the advertising ID where permitted by your device settings, to serve ads. Coins earned from ads are labelled as in-app only and are never redeemable for cash or gift cards.

Personalised advertising and personalised offers are controlled by a separate preference you set when you sign up, which you can change at any time in the app under Profile → Preferences. Turning personalisation off does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised. You can also reset or opt out of advertising-ID-based personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and we honour that choice.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.
  • We do not use your data to train third-party models.
  • We do not ask for, or store, your bank password, UPI PIN, OTP codes, card numbers, or Aadhaar/PAN images. Payouts are made to a UPI ID or bank account you provide, processed by a licensed payment partner.
  • We do not run a credit check or build a credit profile on you.
  • We do not send marketing by SMS unless you have opted in, and any such messaging complies with TRAI DLT registration requirements.

7. Cross-border transfers

Your personal data is stored and processed in India where possible, but some processors operate outside India. In particular, our Neon Postgres database is hosted on AWS in US East 2 (Ohio, USA), and Firebase Authentication and AdMob are operated globally by Google. Data in transit to these services is protected with TLS, with certificate verification enforced for database connections, and providers encrypt stored data at rest.

These transfers are permitted under the DPDP Act 2023 unless the Central Government restricts transfer to a specific country. If you would rather not have your data transferred outside India, do not use the Platform, or write to us and we will explain what this means for your account.

The reward ledger and administrative actions on it are append-only: we never rewrite history. Corrections are made by adding a new entry that reverses an old one, which is why disputed rewards can be traced.

9. How we protect data

  • Transport encryption (TLS) for every network call, with certificate verification enforced against the database provider.
  • Encryption at rest by our infrastructure providers.
  • On-device session tokens encrypted with hardware-backed Android Keystore; nothing sensitive is written to plain local storage.
  • No provider secret, service-account key, database credential, or SMS/email API key is shipped inside the app.
  • Fraud signals are stored as one-way hashes rather than raw values where the raw value is not needed.
  • Server-side verification of every identity claim; clients cannot assert their own user ID, email, phone, balance, or payout status.
  • Rate limiting, short-lived sessions, and session revocation on sign-out.
  • Append-only audit logs for money-moving and administrative actions.
  • Access to production data on a need-to-know basis, with logged administrative actions.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data in a way the law requires us to report, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India without unreasonable delay.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 you may:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the categories of recipients we have shared it with.
  • Correct and complete inaccurate or misleading data — for identity fields such as your phone number this may require re-verification.
  • Erase your data, and withdraw consent, subject to the retention we must observe for financial records, tax, fraud prevention, and legal claims. Withdrawing consent for optional analytics or personalised offers takes effect immediately and does not affect your account.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Grieve — contact us first, then the Grievance Officer, and if still unresolved, the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise a right, use Profile → Support in the app or email [support email] from the address on your account. We verify your identity before responding, and we aim to respond within 30 days. We do not charge for a reasonable request, though we may charge for manifestly unfounded or repetitive ones.

You can also change your analytics and personalisation preferences yourself at any time in the app, and delete your account as described in section 11.

11. Deleting your account

You can request deletion from inside the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the instructions on the account deletion page. Deletion closes the account, forfeits unredeemed coins, and removes or irreversibly anonymises your identity data. We keep only what law requires — principally payout, tax, and fraud-prevention records — and those records are detached from your identity wherever that is possible.
