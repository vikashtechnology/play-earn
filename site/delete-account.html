Delete your account

Available with or without the app installed  ·  Free  ·  Completed within 30 days

Before you delete: deletion closes the account permanently and forfeits any coins you have not redeemed. Any withdrawal or gift-card redemption already in progress will be completed first. If you only want a break, you can sign out instead and keep your balance.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Play & Earn Real Cash and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the summary of what will be removed and what must be retained by law, then confirm.
  4. You are signed out immediately. Your account enters the deletion queue and you receive a confirmation by email.

Option 2 — Delete without the app (web request)

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot open it, request deletion by email. This path is available to everyone and does not require reinstalling anything.

  1. Email [support email] from the email address registered on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request".
  2. In the message, include the email address or phone number on the account. If you no longer have access to that address, tell us and we will verify you another way — for example by confirming your last reward or payout reference.
  3. We acknowledge within 48 hours and confirm once deletion is complete.

You can also use the in-app support ticket (Profile → Support) if you still have the app but prefer not to use the delete button.

What happens when you delete

ItemOutcome
Sign-in sessionsRevoked immediately — existing session tokens stop working.
Profile and identity (name, email, phone, photo)Deleted, or irreversibly anonymised where a record must be kept.
Unredeemed coinsForfeited. Advertising coins and every other balance are removed.
Reward ledger and historyRetained for accounting, tax, and dispute resolution, detached from your identity where possible.
Payout, KYC, and tax recordsRetained for the period Indian payment and tax law requires.
Fraud and sign-in security eventsRetained up to 540 days, then automatically pruned.
Consent recordsRetained to demonstrate what you agreed to and when.
Marketing and analyticsStopped immediately; preferences are removed from active use.

Retention of financial and security records is a legal obligation, not a choice, and it is described in the Privacy Notice. Data we are allowed to delete is deleted or anonymised within 30 days of your request.

Deletion in the authentication provider

Your sign-in identity is held by Firebase Authentication (Google). Deleting your Play & Earn account removes our records; if you also want the underlying Google/Firebase authentication record removed, tell us in the same request and we will remove the provider-side account as well, or you can do it through Google's own tools at myaccount.google.com if you signed in with Google.

Changed your mind?

Before deletion completes, email [support email] with the subject "Cancel deletion request" and we will stop the process. After completion the account cannot be restored: signing up again creates a new, empty account, and forfeited coins cannot be reinstated.

Trouble deleting?

If the app does not offer a working delete option, or we do not acknowledge your request within 48 hours, contact our Grievance Officer — [Grievance Officer name], [grievance email].
